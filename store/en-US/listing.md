## Full description (max 4000)
```
RA Insights turns your RetroAchievements profile into real analysis. Instead of
a list of achievements, you see where you stand, what was genuinely rare in what
you have done, and what is worth playing next.

⚠️ UNOFFICIAL APP, built by a player. Not produced, maintained or endorsed by the
RetroAchievements team.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT IT SHOWS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 DASHBOARD
Points, achievements, day streak and a snapshot of where your account stands —
all on one screen.

💎 RARITY EVERYWHERE
Every achievement carries its real unlock rate and a rarity band (common,
uncommon, rare, very rare, ultra rare). No more guessing what was actually hard.

🏆 YOUR RAREST UNLOCKS
The 10 rarest on your account, filtered by all time, 90 days or 30 days. One tap
opens the achievement.

🎯 ALMOST THERE
The games closest to being finished, ordered by how little is left. One tap takes
you to the game.

🗓️ EVENTS
Every RetroAchievements event in one place, with search, sorting by most recent
and filters for in progress, not started and completed. The app cross-references
events against what you have already unlocked and builds a plan starting with the
easiest achievements.

📚 LIBRARY AND PROGRESSION
Your games by console, with progress bars, points and beaten or mastered state.

👤 PROFILE
The full picture of the account: awards, recent activity and wall comments.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 NO SERVER, NO TRACKING
There is no backend. None of your data passes through any machine belonging to
the developer, because there is none. No ads, no telemetry SDK, no collection.

🔑 YOUR KEY, ON YOUR DEVICE
You sign in with your own username and your own Web API key, taken from your own
RetroAchievements settings page. It is stored encrypted in the Android Keystore
and used only to talk to the official API.

📴 WORKS OFFLINE
After the first load, data is kept on the device. With no connection the app
still opens and shows what it already knows, telling you when it is stale.

🌍 ENGLISH AND PORTUGUESE
Fully translated, following the device language.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT YOU NEED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• A retroachievements.org account
• Your Web API key: retroachievements.org/settings → Keys → Web API Key

The key is read-only. To revoke it, generate a new one on that same page.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RetroAchievements is a trademark of its respective owners. This app only consumes
their public API and has no official affiliation.
```
